Origami-Inspired Lunar Rover

Research in foldable structures, mechanical design, and planetary robotics.

Project Overview

This research explores origami-inspired mechanical structures for compact planetary rover designs. The project investigates folding geometries and deployment mechanisms that could allow rover components to be stowed compactly and expanded for surface operations.

Research Objectives

01

Compact Stowage

Investigate origami-inspired folding geometries to reduce the rover's stowed volume.

02

Mechanical Deployment

Explore mechanisms that transition rover structures between folded and deployed configurations.

03

CAD Development

Develop and evaluate foldable rover geometries using Onshape.

Mechanical Design & CAD Development

My work focuses on developing origami-inspired folding geometries using Onshape. I am exploring how individual panels, mechanical connections, and geometric constraints influence the rover's ability to transition between compact and deployed configurations.

Engineering Challenges & Design Decisions

Developing a foldable rover requires balancing geometric constraints, mechanical connections, and deployment motion.

Maintaining Folding Motion Across Connected Panels

During CAD development, individual folding panels demonstrated the intended motion. However, connecting the upper and lower structures introduced assembly constraints that caused the structures to separate during folding.
